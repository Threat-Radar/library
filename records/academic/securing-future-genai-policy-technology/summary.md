## Overview

Per RPT-0009 §2.7: the report of an October 2023 workshop by the organizers
who later ran SAGAI. It compares GenAI regulation (the EU AI Act, the US
Executive Order, China's rules) with what technology can deliver, finds limits
in alignment, model inspection and watermarking, and recommends research into
out-of-model guardrails, the same topics as the SAGAI'24 call for papers.
