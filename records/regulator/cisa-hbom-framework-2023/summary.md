## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** Voluntary CISA guidance, written by the HBOM Working Group of the ICT Supply Chain Risk Management Task Force, for describing the hardware components of a product to purchasers. It sets out use-case categories (compliance, security, availability), a parent-child nesting format for assemblies, and a taxonomy of 47 HBOM data fields in seven categories, each mapped where possible to a CycloneDX and an SPDX field; a two-page fact sheet summarises it. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the main public HBOM guidance: its 47-field taxonomy, use-case categories, nesting layout and CycloneDX and SPDX mappings (report section 2, Table 4).
