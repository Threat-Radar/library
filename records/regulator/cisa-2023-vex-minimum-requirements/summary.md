## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** A CISA-published document that defines, independently of any format, the minimum data elements of a VEX document: document and statement metadata, product and vulnerability details, and one status per statement (not affected, affected, fixed or under investigation), with the impact statement, justification or action statement that some statuses require. It also covers optional elements and notes on references, metadata inheritance, cryptography and identity. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): CISA's four VEX statuses (§2.7.1), the reference column of RPT-0004's VEX status comparison.
