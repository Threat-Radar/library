## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** CERT-In's Technical Guidelines, version 2.0, advise government, public sector and essential services organisations on producing and using SBOMs and extend the guidance to quantum and cryptographic BOMs (QBOM and CBOM), AI BOMs (AIBOM) and hardware BOMs (HBOM). They give a minimum element table for each bill type, a syntax for unique component identifiers, SPDX or CycloneDX as formats, and a VEX and CSAF process for vulnerability tracking. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): a national SBOM baseline and the only national HBOM element list found (Table 11), for RPT-0004 dimensions 2, 8 and 9.
