## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** Part 2 of BSI's Technical Guideline TR-03183 sets formal and technical requirements for SBOMs in preparation for the EU Cyber Resilience Act: accepted formats and minimum versions (CycloneDX 1.6 or later, SPDX 3.0.1 or later), required, additional and optional data fields for the SBOM and for each component, the depth of dependency resolution, and licence identifiers and expressions. Version 2.1.0 adds an informative section mapping its data fields to CycloneDX and SPDX. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): a national SBOM baseline in RPT-0004 dimension 8: required fields, accepted format versions, and the rule that an SBOM must not carry vulnerability information.
