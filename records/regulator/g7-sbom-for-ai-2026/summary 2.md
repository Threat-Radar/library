## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** Joint guidance from the G7 Cybersecurity Working Group listing the minimum elements an SBOM for an AI system should contain in addition to the general SBOM minimum elements, grouped into seven clusters: metadata, system level properties, models, datasets, infrastructure, security properties and key performance indicators. It builds on the G7 shared vision on SBOM for AI of June 2025 and states that the elements are not mandatory and create no requirements, standards or legislation. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): existing government guidance on AI SBOM elements, for RPT-0004 dimension 9 (other bill types); Phase 1 knew it only from CISA's bulletin.
