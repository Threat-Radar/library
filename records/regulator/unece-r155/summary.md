## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** UN Regulation No. 155 sets uniform provisions for the type approval of road vehicles with regard to cyber security, including requirements for the manufacturer's cyber security management system and an annex listing threats and corresponding mitigations; it applies to categories M and N, to category O with at least one electronic control unit, and to L6 and L7 with automated driving from level 3. This record holds the original version, in force since 22 January 2021 and published by UNECE in March 2021. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the observation that the regulation does not mention SBOMs or bills of materials.
