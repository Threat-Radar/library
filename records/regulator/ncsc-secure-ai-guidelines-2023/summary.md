## Overview

Joint guidance from the UK and US cyber agencies, with international partners,
"for providers of any systems that use artificial intelligence (AI), whether
those systems have been created from scratch or built on top of tools and
services provided by others" (NCSC page). The page groups the guidelines by
life-cycle stage, from secure design to secure deployment and beyond, and
says they follow the secure-by-design principles published by CISA, the NCSC
and international agencies. Only the publication details and landing page were
checked for this stub.
