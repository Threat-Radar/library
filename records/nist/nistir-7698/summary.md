## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** NIST IR 7698 specifies the CPE 2.3 applicability language: applicability statements that combine CPE names and references to checks with AND, OR and negation to describe the IT platforms to which guidance or policies apply. It also sets conformance requirements for products and applicability language documents. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): one point: applicability statements combine CPE names only with logical operators (AND, OR, negation) and define no version range.
