## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** NIST IR 7697 specifies CPE 2.3 dictionaries: the data model and management rules for repositories of CPE names, including the Official CPE Dictionary that NIST hosts as the authoritative source of identifier names, extended dictionaries, and the deprecation process by which names are corrected or replaced. It also sets conformance requirements for dictionaries and the products that use them. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the authority of the Official CPE Dictionary over identifier names, and the rule that names change only through deprecation.
