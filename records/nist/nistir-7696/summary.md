## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** NIST IR 7696 specifies CPE 2.3 name matching: a one-to-one comparison of a source CPE name with a target CPE name, attribute by attribute, that reports whether the names are equal, one is a superset or subset of the other, or they are disjoint. It also sets conformance requirements for products that implement the matching. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): one definition (one-to-one comparison of two CPE names) and the absence of version ranges or version ordering in CPE matching.
