Fraunhofer IESE and co-located with the IEEE International Conference on
Software Architecture (ICSA). It shares the acronym SAGAI with the IEEE S&P
workshop series on securing generative AI (`sagai-workshop`), which is the one
tmodel's RPT-0009 studies.
