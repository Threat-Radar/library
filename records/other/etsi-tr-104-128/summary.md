## Overview

The implementation guide that accompanies the baseline requirements (TS 104 223,
now EN 304 223). Its bookmarks show a section of examples for meeting the
provisions, one per principle (6.1 to 6.13), and an annex mapping design and
organization principles to SAI. The UK government's Code of Practice page says
the UK's implementation guide was submitted to ETSI as its basis.
