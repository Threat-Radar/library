## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** DMTF standard that defines the messages, data objects and sequences two devices exchange over various transports and physical media, including authentication of hardware identities and firmware measurements; mappings to particular transports are defined in other DMTF specifications. It gives information for enforcing security policy but leaves the policy decisions themselves out of scope. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the measurement value types and the hardware identity carried in device certificates (Table 4b; signed measurements imported as Assertions by the device).
