## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** A Linux Foundation Research report that explains how to build AI and dataset bills of materials with the SPDX 3.0 AI and Dataset profiles. It compares the profiles with datasheets, model cards and FactSheets, discusses their use for the EU AI Act, FDA and EMA requirements for medical devices and the IEEE P70xx standards, describes each mandatory and optional AIPackage and DatasetPackage field, and closes with worked examples.
