## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** An Informational RFC that retires the MD2 message digest algorithm and moves RFC 1319, which specified it, to Historic status. It gives the rationale (published collision, preimage and second preimage attacks on MD2) and lists the RFCs that had specified the use of MD2. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the IETF's move of MD2 to Historic status, cited in RPT-0004 section 1.2 on SPDX's hash algorithm list.
