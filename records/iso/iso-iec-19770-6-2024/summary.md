## Overview

**A stub: not read in full.** ISO/IEC 19770-6:2024 specifies hardware identification (HWID) tags, a transport format for identification data about hardware devices or components, presented as the hardware counterpart of SWID tags for tag producers and tag consumers such as device providers and IT discovery tools. Its contents list covers primary and system HWID types, uniqueness of identifiers, XML and JSON digital signatures, minimum and recommended tag data, data definitions and an informative XSD. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): the ISO hardware identification tag standard mentioned, but not read, in RPT-0004 section 2 on HBOMs.
